Privacy Policy for PolicyHub

Last Updated: November 6, 2025

1. Introduction

PolicyHub ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application PolicyHub (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

The App allows you to store the following information locally on your device:

  • Agent Information: Name, agent code, phone number (stored locally for PDF quotations)
  • Customer Information: Name, phone number, email, date of birth, gender (stored locally for quotation purposes)
  • Quotation Data: Insurance policy details and premium calculations (stored locally)
  • App Settings: Theme preferences, PIN, biometric authentication settings (stored locally)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

The App uses Google AdMob for advertising, which may collect:

  • Device information (device ID, model, operating system)
  • Advertising ID
  • App usage data for ad personalization
  • Location data (if permitted) for ad targeting

Note: This data is collected by Google AdMob, not by us. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Local Storage: All customer and agent data is stored locally on your device using secure storage methods. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it (e.g., via WhatsApp or email).
  • Premium Calculations: All calculations are performed locally on your device. No calculation data is transmitted to external servers.
  • PDF Generation: PDFs are generated locally using the information you provide and are only shared when you explicitly choose to share them.
  • Advertising: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use collected data to show relevant ads.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All data is stored locally on your device using:
    • AsyncStorage for general app data
    • SecureStore (hardware-backed encryption) for sensitive data like PINs
  • No External Servers: We do not operate any external servers. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the "Purge Data" option in app settings.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google AdMob

Our App uses Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob may collect and use information as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

5.2 Sharing Features

The App allows you to share quotations via:

  • WhatsApp (uses WhatsApp's sharing mechanism)
  • Email (uses your device's email app)
  • Other sharing apps on your device

We do not control how these third-party services handle your data. Please review their privacy policies.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access Your Data: All data is stored locally on your device. You can view it within the app.
  • Delete Your Data: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or using the "Purge Data" option in settings.
  • Opt Out of Ads: You can disable personalized ads in your device's Google settings.
  • Biometric Authentication: You can enable or disable biometric authentication in app settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
